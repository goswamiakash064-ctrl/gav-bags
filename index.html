ABOUT GAV BAGS

Made for everyday life.

Since 2020, GAV Bags has been crafting quality Jute & Canvas bags designed for everyday life.

We started our journey in 2020 with a simple vision — to create bags that are durable, practical, stylish and suitable for everyday use. Over the years, we have built a strong presence in the local markets of West Bengal, including Esplanade, Gariahat and Digha, while also expanding our supply to other markets and states.

Today, GAV Bags also supplies a wide range of Jute & Canvas bags to the Puri market in Odisha, serving customers who value quality, functionality and attractive designs.

Our collection covers a wide variety of bags for different needs — from everyday tote bags, handbags and shopping bags to office bags, casual bags, tiffin bags, bottle bags, carry bags and utility bags.

We continuously develop designs that fit into the daily lifestyle of modern customers, whether for work, shopping, travel, gifting or casual use.

At GAV Bags, we believe a bag should be more than just something you carry. It should be functional, comfortable, durable and stylish enough to complement your lifestyle.

From simple everyday essentials to thoughtfully designed lifestyle bags, we aim to provide Jute & Canvas bags for every person, every purpose and every occasion.
